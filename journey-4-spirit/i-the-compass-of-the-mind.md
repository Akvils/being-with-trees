#### Original Sin

Everyone is born innocent. A newborn baby is sinless, as it has not done anything.

Before he was born, people turned away from the divine presence and their divine nature. They became sinful, missing the point that they have to be the embodiment of the divine.

A baby is born innocent, but he is born in sin. The society and environment in which they are born is sinful.&#x20;

There is lust, anger, immorality, greed, hypnotic trance, delusion and other evils in their family, neighbourhood and environment.  Sometimes dark entities enter them. In some cases, the evil is so much that witches put such things in them.

And that sin enters them, they absorb all these evils, and lose touch with their divine nature and contact with divinity.

#### The Loss of Innocence

Little children sometimes see monsters and strange things. Because they are still innocent, their awareness is unclouded by the physical world. They see with spiritual eyes, perceiving things that adults have lost the ability to see.

As we grow older, we begin to fuse our spiritual selves with our physical selves. We lose our innocence, and with it, our spiritual vision.

We become gross, thickened by the animal self. Have you ever felt that your hands had suddenly become enormous? A strange bigness throughout your body, a sense of thickness, awkwardness, or clumsiness takes over. As though you had become heavier, less subtle, less aware.

As innocence fades, we become increasingly identified with our animal nature. We become self-conscious, absorbed in our bodies, thoughts, and feelings. In a sense, we begin to die in the spirit and come to life as animals, procreating creatures bound to the physical world, rather than eternally renewing spirits.

When we are children, however, our innocence allows us to perceive things beyond the immediate physical world. Through the light of one spiritual world, we may glimpse another. We may see monsters, sense evil on the other side, or feel that something is trying to reach us from beyond what others can perceive.

Meditation brings us back towards that original innocence. As we become less identified with the body, thoughts, and feelings, we may begin to perceive what was previously hidden from us. We begin to confront the horror within ourselves, the darkness in the world around us, and the folds of our own sensuality.

We fuse our spiritual selves, our consciousness, with the flesh. We begin to see like brutes, like animals, rather than through the clarity of an awakened human being. In doing so, we can feel comfortable with ourselves, because we no longer see ourselves clearly.

The more deeply we become absorbed in our desires and sensations, the more we learn to stimulate and satisfy ourselves in ways that drown out our conscience. We lose ourselves in our thoughts and feelings, allowing them to obscure what we have become. Our conscience grows quiet, and we cease to recognise the nature of the people and influences we surround ourselves with. Those who appear to be our friends may not be what they seem.

But when we meditate, we begin to awaken. We become more capable of seeing ourselves honestly, recognising our own darkness, and discerning the nature of the influences around us. We may begin to see our supposed friends differently, not merely as they appear to us, but as we understand them through a clearer and more conscious awareness.

#### The Internal Compass

Inside every person is a compass needle. It is never still. It moves, shifts, searches, and rarely settles. But wherever it stops, that is the realm your mind connects to.

When someone lets their needle point toward hell, their mind becomes like the mind of the devil. Their heart knots itself to darkness, and before they even realise it, their life fills with struggle, confusion, and destruction. They walk straight into it because the needle led them there.

When the needle stops in the direction of lust, the mind plugs directly into the rot of lust. Through that opening, the filth behind lust, its ghosts, its parasites, its predators, snakes, forces its way in. They take over the mind. And people under that influence become attached to the opposite sex in a way that isn’t even human anymore. They become nothing more than tools to satisfy the hunger of those ghosts.

But those who hold pure thoughts, whose needle points toward the good, toward the light of heaven, they are different. Their minds connect upward. The ones in the higher realms watch them with joy, with recognition. Their hearts feel peace while still alive, because their needle is aligned with the realm of peace.

There are also those who think only of helping others, without pride or show, who want nothing but to walk the righteous path. Their minds stay in tune with the heavenly realm. Peace becomes natural to them.

The needle of the mind moves in ways we cannot see. But wherever it rests, it will lead a person straight to heaven or straight to hell. One thought can open three thousand worlds. Heaven and hell begin inside the mind.

True enlightenment is found only in quiet, in harmony, in a mind that has stopped scattering itself. You must steady the needle inside you. Direct it toward the divine. Keep it pointing to the One who is above all.

Point the needle toward the divine presence so that we consciously absorb the light as we unconsciously absorbed the darkness growing up.
