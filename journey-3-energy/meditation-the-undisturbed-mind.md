# 🧘‍♂️ Meditation: The Undisturbed Mind

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/ChatGPT Image Dec 7, 2025, 11_02_38 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Part 1: Foundation by the Tree

Take your seat under the tree. Settle in, allowing your back to rest against its trunk. Feel the now-familiar support of the earth beneath you and the tree behind you.

Establish your posture, letting the tree help you keep your spine comfortably straight. Rest your hands upon your lap. Bring that subtle, inner smile to your lips and, when you are ready, gently close your eyes.

Now, bring your awareness inward and upward to the space between your eyebrows. Let your attention settle here. This is the calm centre from which you will observe. Rest here for a moment. Watch the vast space of your inner sky.

(Pause)

#### Part 2: Awakening the Body

From your centre of observation at the third eye, become very aware of yourself, yourself within yourself. What thoughts are on your mind? What feelings are present? Simply notice them for a moment.

Now, let your awareness travel down to your hands.

Bring your full attention to your hands. Feel them resting in your lap. Feel the blood flowing into them. Feel the warmth, the aliveness, as the blood rushes into them. Bring your attention to your hands until they feel as though they are completely alive.

(Short Pause)

Now, expand this feeling of aliveness. Let it travel up from your hands into your arms. Feel your forearms, your elbows, and your upper arms, all humming with this same warm, alive energy.

Bring this awareness up to your shoulders, and then let it spread across your entire upper torso. Feel your chest and your upper back becoming alive with sensation.

Let the awareness travel down your spine and fill your entire abdomen and lower back. Feel this core part of you become warm and present.

Now, bring your attention down to your legs. Feel your thighs, your knees, your calves, and your shins. Let the blood flow, let the tingling come. Make them feel alive with your attention. Don’t force it. The body responds to your awareness.

Finally, bring your full awareness to your feet, resting on the earth. Feel the soles of your feet, your heels, your toes. Feel them tingle with life, connected to the ground.

Now, feel your entire body at once, from the top of your head to the tips of your toes, as one complete field of warm, vibrating aliveness.

Hold this feeling of total body awareness. Keep doing this, feeling the aliveness of the body, as we proceed.

#### Part 3: Watching the Mind

You are aware of your entire body, a complete field of warm, tingling aliveness. Keep this feeling.

Now, as though you are looking out from the middle of your forehead, turn your attention to the inner space where thoughts, distractions, and the chatter of the mind arise. Do not strain your eyes; just observe this space.

Here, we will create a cycle, a force field of awareness. The two points of this field are: the space in your forehead where you observe, and the tingling aliveness of your body.

Let thoughts come out of hiding. Let them appear.

As a thought arises, simply gaze upon it from your observation post. Don't engage with it. Don't fight it. Just see it like clouds passing by.

Then, as you see the thought, immediately extend or expand your concentration back to the aliveness of your body. Feel the tingling in your hands, the warmth in your arms, the life in your legs. This creates a pulsation of energy.

The cycle is simple: Watch the thought. Feel the body.

Observe the thought from your forehead, and then extend that awareness until it becomes a aliveness in your body.

(Short Pause)

As you do this, you may become aware of subtle patterns of light in your vision. If a particularly distracting thought arises, you can gently superimpose these light patterns over the thought. This will help to dissolve it. Then, let your awareness proceed back to the aliveness of your body.

If your mind wanders off completely and you forget the practice, the moment you realise it, just bring your attention back. Gently pull back.

Watch your thoughts. Feel the aliveness of your body.

By doing this again and again, those nagging thoughts will begin to disappear and lose their power. Your awareness will grow as a living force from within, stronger than the pressures from the outside.

We will now rest in this practice. Observe the thoughts from the middle of your forehead, and then extend that awareness to the tingling aliveness in your body.

(Long, deep silence for practice)

#### Part 4: The Journey of Wisdom

Keep concentrating now, even though you are hearing these words. Let them be in the background. My voice is not important. Make the concentration more important. Look through the middle of your forehead and extend that awareness so it becomes aliveness in your body.

As you do, let this understanding unfold.

(Short Pause)

#### Section 1: The Foundation of Peace

That thought about the past… no matter what it was, it shouldn’t feel important. There is nothing you can do in your thinking to change it. So, forget it. What you don't think about cannot bother you.

The future is similar. It is not here. Do not worry about it.

Clear your mind and keep your thoughts in the present. Be present.

From this place of clarity, understand this: from now onwards, nothing must upset, irritate, aggravate, agitate, or hurt your feelings. Not in the slightest degree. Especially those little dishonest, unkind, unfair things that people say and do to you and others from day-to-day. You must not be annoyed, inwardly or outwardly.

Don’t suppress your anger. But remember to overlook and make allowances right at that moment. Do this not because you have to, but because you want to.

Realise it is your own anger and irritation that hurt you more than other people. Therefore, from now onward, look for the opportunity to overlook and forgive right on the spot. Immediately when it happens, not two seconds later. This is how you respond more and more to what you know is right, and less and less to conditions and people.

Make allowances for everyone from now onwards, no matter who it is. But start with those closest to you. First make allowances for your family, your close relations. Because you cannot control your own faults, the ones you can see and the ones you cannot see, you do not have the right to be annoyed at the faults of others. If you cannot do it for them, how do you expect to do it for strangers? On the other hand, if you make allowances for them, it will be easy to do it for people who mean less to you.

Do not expect anything from anyone. When you expect something and do not get makes you annoyed. It doesn't matter what others have or do.

When you do something for someone, do it because you want to, not because you have to.

Finally, you must overlook praise just as you would overlook an offence. Do not be uplifted by it. Do not react to it or absorb it. Do not allow it to lift you and pump you up. Stay calm. Be yourself.

Continue the practice. Watch the thought. Feel the body.

#### Section 2: The Path of Action

Stop having any imaginary conversations with people about what you are going to say to so-and-so next time you see them. Don’t mind what you should have done or could have said. What has been cannot be changed, no matter how much you wish it. But you can change from now on.

If you have felt disturbed about some worry or mistake in the past, remember this: a good person would forgive you without your asking. And if they are not a good person, just make allowances for them anyway. Each time you overlook, you undo a little of what you have done in the form of mistakes yourself.

Understand the nature of bravery. The one who fails to be brave is a coward, filled with remorse. But by choosing to be courageous in the next moment, that cowardice is erased. If the world suddenly became perfect, the coward would never have the chance to redeem himself.

So with you. You have allowed yourself to become annoyed and irritated over many trivial things. When you could have overlooked and made allowances, you did not.

Therefore, be glad when people are rude or unkind, for here is your opportunity to overlook. If there was no danger, you could not possess courage. If there was no hate, how could you develop love? These individuals are giving you the opportunity to accomplish what you failed to do before. The situation which once made you upset and unhappy will become the very thing to give you happiness and well-being.

Whoever tries to anger you is giving you the opportunity to rise above your problem. They do not know this, but they are doing you a great service. And the harder people try to upset you, the calmer you must become.

Therefore, remember to overlook right on the spot, and then be plain spoken with firmness, kindness, and patience.

As long as you are not annoyed, you can always disagree without being disagreeable. If people are upset because of your honesty, they are not your friends anyway, and you might as well know it now. Your real friends will respect and love you for your honesty and truthfulness.

Keep doing the exercise. Watch the thought. Feel the body.

#### Section 3: The Motivation

Keep this idea running through your thoughts often: Overlook and be plain spoken. Have the awareness of it at all times.

Let this be your spiritual and moral goal. Let it be more important than any material goal in this world, for it is the means by which everything else can be accomplished.

Let your emphasis be on desiring to see truly, on being fair and honest with others and yourself, and on standing corrected without making excuses. Do not let your mind be cluttered with worldly worries or thoughts of gain, love, power, or riches. Good things will come when your desire for living truly and justly takes first place over everything.

Let meditation and being your true self give you the satisfaction and joy, which will far exceed the pleasure of material things. It should be a pleasure to think about it, to understand it, and to do it again and again.

Let this idea change everything that passes through your mind, so that you make decisions according to it. Let it shape everything you feel, do, or say, so that it conforms to the practice: to overlook on the spot and be outspoken with firmness, kindness, and patience.

Do it because you want to, not because you have to.\
Do it because you want understanding.\
Do it to think good, rather than to feel good\*.\
Do it for the pure love of doing and being a better person, whether it makes you well or not.

And if you want to have an objective to think about, think: "If I could just learn to make allowances for people, I'd be better off." As this is the truth. No other goal will give you satisfaction, so there is no point in thinking about it.

Everything being said here merely points back to this simple instruction. The purpose of these words is to help you understand how and when to overlook. This is very important. You must remember it often. Do your exercise on your own, and you will find it will keep this thought alive again and again.

Keep doing the exercise. Watch the thought. Feel the body.

#### Section 4: The Error of Judgment

Whatever personal problem you have, stop looking for its reason. All you do is confuse yourself more. Keep it out of your thoughts by thinking about what is being taught here. Do not analyze what is being said; think about it. Do not doubt; push such thoughts out of your mind. Just keep remembering the basic idea: to overlook on the spot and be plain spoken.

Give up suspicion as a defence. You became suspicious only when you trusted without light. You became too trusting only when you lost faith in your own judgment and were forced to depend on others.

Learn to accept and reject calmly. Do not work up anger to reject. Do not work up a head of steam of excitement to learn or study. Just watch calmly. What is valuable will stay with you, and you will automatically forget what is useless and meaningless.

Discern, don’t judge. When you get rid of anger and excitement, your judgment becomes a healthy discerning. You are judging when you are angry. Remember that. Anger spoils the observation and taints it, making it unhealthy. Observe your hostility over the failings of others. Is it not an unhealthy ego need to look superior? Anger and secret resentment only excite and distract you from seeing what is wrong with you.

Give up judgment then, calm down, and you will see your own faults come to light. That is all right.

Do not study intellectually. The sheer weight of information cannot make you better. You cannot learn the truth, but you can absorb it, as a flower does the sun. See the meaning behind the words. Do not force understanding. Do not be impatient with it. Just hold the words and the experience, and in time, they will become a key to awaken you to their meaning.

Continue the practice. Watch the thought. Feel the body.

#### Section 5: The Engine of Change

Understand this of yourself: you can do nothing. You cannot even make a decision to be right in a moment of fear. Do not be so proud as to think that you can free yourself from the clutches of error and temptation.

You can only desire. Desire, hunger and thirst after righteousness. Continuously ponder, wonder, ask questions, and question yourself. Yearn for the privilege of being able to think and speak in the right way, naturally, without analysis, without scheming and planning.

And when you are finally able to choose, understand that it will not be by your own power, but through the strength of grace, by which you are again given the power to live correctly and to rule in your body. For this, be thankful. Until then, be patient.

Regardless of how pure your motives are at this moment, they may not always have been. We have all been tested, and our secret, ambitious nature was revealed. Choice by choice, mistake by mistake, we have built a proud, judging, self-justifying nature, a nature that has been loving only to be exalted in return. Hopefully, you will come to see this.

This dreaming, ambitious, goal-seeking, selfish, now tortured, lustful nature must be sorry and petition for that last chance to make right and fair decisions.

This is what true repentance is: do not just be sorry for yourself. That was the old way of feeling better than your conscience. Instead, desire only to be able to live differently. For your persistent problems, feel the pain of not being able to choose rightly. Let that pain grow until it becomes a true repentance, a real need. There is value in a real need. Do not stifle it with pleasure or comfort.

This precious need, this humility, makes you acceptable, and enables compassion to be extended to you. And this compassion, for you, is your discovery of being able to make a right choice effortlessly. This leads to true gratefulness, and to a procession of joys.

Seeing your own unworthiness, you may become worthy of grace. If you think yourself worthy, you will only expect a reward, and there will be nothing but frustration for this vanity.

Keep doing the exercise. Watch the thought. Feel the body.

#### Section 6: The Experience of the Path

As you grow, you will uncover new layers of faults. This is often preceded by a flurry of meaningless thoughts and some of the same pain as before. This is a process that takes time. Just keep observing and being sorry in the new way. Soon, they too will change.

As your awareness becomes more aware, insight grows to see your errors. Be aware of them in the light of new knowledge. Do not resent what comes to light. Bear the pain of your failings graciously. Do not blame anyone. Be aware of your part in the past and present troubles, noting the compulsiveness of your own behaviour. This is how you change.

Observe the pains, the tensions, the emotions in your body. Each pain, each ache, tells a story of a wrong way you have reacted to life, to people, and to things. Do not try to change what you see. Just be aware of what calls your attention and observe it. Do not escape from the facts before you, inside or out. Bear the discomfort. Observe it graciously, without anger or fear. Be equanimous.&#x20;

Do not expect an instant reward for what you are going through. Expect nothing, or else you make grace impossible. If you have no results and you are angry, realise your wrong motive and your play-acting for reward.

Do not cling to old experiences or new revelations. Just keep being aware.

Do not depend on memories, no matter how meaningful they are, to decide your next action or word. Wait. Know that you do not know. Admit it. Then desire to know. Feel the need to know. Be quiet in the process of analysis and worry, and then you will know. Sometimes, doing nothing is the right answer. Be patient.

Resist the tendency to doubt. You cannot make yourself believe, but you can stop yourself from doubting.

Do not be hung up on these words, this lesson, anyone, or anything. Do not depend on anything. Meditate for the strength. Your crutches will fall away.

The light of meaning will give you new direction. Your new development is answerable to the power of good that is making you new. But for you, it will be effortless and easy, like a light was shining on your path. You will gasp as an observer in wonder and become renewed in faith.

This is the path.

#### Part 5: The Conclusion

Now, let the words and ideas dissolve.\
Let the cycle of watching and feeling come to rest.

Bring your awareness fully back to the body sitting here. Feel the solid trunk of the tree against your back. Feel the texture of the earth beneath you. Listen to the sounds of the world around you.

Now, begin slowly to open your eyes. Arise. Give yourself a good, long stretch.
